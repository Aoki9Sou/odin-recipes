Strawberry Orange Banana Smoothie


This orange juice smoothie with banana and strawberries is super refreshing! Made with super easy to get ingredients and time efficient

  • 1 cup fresh strawberries
  • ¾ cup orange juice
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup ice cubes, or as needed

  1. Step 1 Blend strawberries, orange juice, banana, and ice in a blender until smooth and frothy
